Claim boundary

The abstract bridge is not a publication theorem

The existing PNP.PEqualsNP structure uses string handles for languages and witness code. It is trivially inhabitable and explicitly publication-ineligible. The finite charged-pipeline target PNP.Main.ConcretePEqualsNP and raw-machine linkage are formalized, but reviewed activation fingerprints and compatibility root PNP.Main.p_eq_np remain absent.
